If you stay in Woodlands and your toddler cannot sit still, this one is quite useful. Go all the way up to Level 7 of Causeway Point. On the roof there is a small outdoor water playground, full of cartoon dinosaurs, water jets, and a volcano with slides. No ticket, no minimum spend. Just walk in.

Do not expect Adventure Cove, or even a big splash pad. The whole place is compact. For a two to five year old, that is actually better. You can see them the whole time, and they will still come out soaked and happy.

## The toddler side

The quieter corner is the one for the smaller kids. There is a rib cage made of thick colourful bones, about two metres high, with a yellow bone hanging in the middle like a swing. Let them sit on it only. Do not let them stand or climb it. Water mist comes down inside, so they feel like they are in some dino cave.

Beside that, a red baby T-rex spits a small jet of water, and there is a row of cracked eggs in green, orange, and red. Water pours from one shell into the next, with little wheels and levers. That is the right kind of play for a toddler. Low, slow, and they can poke at it for a long time without you having to hover on a slide.

## The volcano and the slides

The main show is the volcano. Every few minutes water dumps down the front, with a bit of mist, and all the bigger kids will scream and run in together. Very standard splash-bucket behaviour, just with lava paint on it.

Two slides come off it. One is an open orange water slide. The other is a red tube that stays drier. Stairs at the back go up into the volcano, and there is a rope climb on the side. These are really for about five and up. A confident four year old might manage with you right there, but the steps are not toddler height, and the floor around the jets gets slippery. There is also a tall blue long-neck that sprays water, and ground jets the kids can step on to set off.

## The honest bit

It is free, and it shows in the size. One good session is enough. After that they are repeating the same three things. Weekend late morning can be packed, and people do queue before opening. The sign says 45 minutes of play, then 15 minutes to clear out, about 80 children at a time. On a quiet weekday they sometimes do not really chase you. On a packed Sunday, do not argue. Just go.

It is outdoors. From eleven to three, the sun is no joke and the rubber floor can feel hot. Weekday morning, or after five, is much more pleasant. After seven the lights come on and it looks quite nice. If it rains heavily, or there is lightning, they close. Every second Monday of the month is maintenance, or the next day if that Monday is a public holiday. It has also shut for longer stretches before, so if you are coming from far, check before you leave. A few parents have waited at a locked gate past 10am while staff finish the water check. A bit frustrating if you planned the morning around it.

The shower is only a small open corner for rinsing. There is no proper changing room. Rinse off, then change in the toilet just inside the mall. Bring a towel, spare clothes, and slippers. A swimsuit is safer. Normal clothes also can, but you will be carrying a plastic bag of wet shirt.

Seats for parents are there, and you can actually sit and watch, which a lot of water playgrounds do not give you. When it is full, the benches fill up fast. Because the area is small, standing at the side still gives you a clear view. The food court is on the same floor, so after they change you can eat without dragging a wet child down to the basement.

One more thing. The soft padding is not everywhere. The bare bits are slippery. Hold the smaller ones near the jets.

## Worth the trip?

If you are already at Causeway Point, yes. If you stay in the north and want somewhere free to kill an hour while the kid burns energy, also yes. If you are driving from the east just for this, maybe not. It is a neighbourhood water play, not a destination park. For what it is, I would still bring a toddler back. Free, dinosaur, water, and a place to sit. That combination is rare in Singapore.

## Before you go

- **Where:** Causeway Point, 1 Woodlands Square, Level 7 roof. Woodlands MRT (NS9/TE2), Exit 2.
- **When:** about 10am to 9pm. Closed every second Monday, and when the weather is bad.
- **Who:** 12 and below. The toddler zone suits about 2 to 5. The slides suit about 5 to 12.
- **Price:** free.
- **Bring:** towel, a change of clothes, slippers, and a plastic bag. Sunscreen if you are going in the day.

Hours, the 45-minute session, and the monthly closure are the rules the mall publishes for this playground. Confirm on the day if you are making a special trip. The place has been closed for longer maintenance before.
