I wrote about [Jsecret Store](https://jsecretstore.com) in September as a website: 925 sterling silver, boxes ready for gifts, a parcel that can reach Singapore. The new posters do a different job. On Saturday 21 November 2026 the brand is standing in a room at someone else's anniversary, with samples on a dish and a QR code on the sheet, so people notice it is there.

The posters are for **PEWA's 4th anniversary**. The jewellery sheet calls the afternoon **HER Era**. The wider sheet calls it **HER Trade**, 她时代, and "Creating Her Era, Together." Jsecret Store is paired with **Xplora**. One line on the jewellery sheet is crystal silver jewellery. The line beside it is an alcohol ink workshop. The hours, on the sheet that prints them, are **12pm to 6pm** at **Loft 29, Penang**.

## Quick take

This is an appearance. The poster says **samples on display**. It also tells you to scan if you want to browse the jewellery, and it prints **jsecretstore.com** on the artwork. That split is the part worth keeping. You can see a few pieces in the room. The catalogue you order from is the website, which is also where the free shipping rules live.

Free shipping on the [published shipping policy](https://jsecretstore.com/policies/shipping-policy), checked 30 October 2026:

- West Malaysia: orders above **RM 150**
- East Malaysia: orders above **RM 200**
- Singapore: orders above **RM 300**

Under those lines the same page lists Peninsular Malaysia at **RM 6** (1 to 3 days), Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan at **RM 12** (5 to 7 days), and Singapore at **SGD 8** (5 to 7 days). Parcels go out within **1 to 3 working days** after payment.

## Samples in the room, catalogue on the site

**Samples on display** means the dish is a notice. A bangle, a pendant, and a pair of studs can show finish, weight, and colour. The baby bangles, the zodiac bracelet, the gourd and abacus pieces, the hoops, and the rest of the shelves stay on the site.

The poster invites a scan "to browse thousands of jewellery pieces." On 30 October 2026 a catalogue search on the public site returned **695** results. The poster line is an invitation. The search count is what I could open that day. Both send you to the same place: the range on [jsecretstore.com](https://jsecretstore.com/collections/all), which is why the QR is on the sheet.

If a sample appeals, the next page is the product page: length, weight, the hallmark note, and whether the SKU is on sale. Sale items sat outside the return window when I reviewed the shop. That line is not on this poster, so it still lives on the product page and in the [September review](/reviews/honest-review-jsecret-store).

Go if you are in George Town that Saturday and you want the brand in front of you for a minute: metal in the hand, then a decision on your phone. The QR is that second step. The full catalogue, the measurements, and the free shipping are on the website after you scan.
