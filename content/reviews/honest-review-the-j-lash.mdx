I already had a lash artist I was happy with. A friend who films a lot still sent me to **[The J Lash](https://www.instagram.com/the_jlash/)**, and I have stayed. The sets hold up when someone else is holding the camera, and they still check in after you leave the chair.

The studio is in the north of Singapore, at Woodlands Ave 9. On Instagram the bio writes the road as "Woodland Ave 9", with a Singapore flag and a Penang flag beside it, and the profile location is Woodlands Glade. The number on the profile is **+65 8176 8498**.

## Quick take

**The J Lash** is a lash studio. The work is extensions and lash lifts, and the profile lists the artist as a certified lash artist. A lot of the regulars are people who are on camera. Creators keep the same chair because the mapping is done for how lashes read on a phone, including when you look slightly down at a screen.

If you live in Woodlands, Sembawang, or Admiralty, or you are crossing from Johor Bahru, this is an easy booking. Town is the longer trip. If you live in the east, that trip is the real cost of the appointment.

## Why I keep the same artist

**The set is built for camera.** Fans stay separated. The outer corner is mapped so it holds when you glance down, which is how most of us look at a phone. People who film treat that as the requirement. If a set only looks tidy in the mirror, they do not come back.

**The materials feel consistent from visit to visit.** I judge them as the person wearing them. The fans are light. They do not feel scratchy by the evening. The curl still looks intentional after a humid afternoon. When a few lashes feel tight, or one eye looks heavier, they would rather you come back than sort it out at home with tweezers.

**Follow-up is part of the appointment.** After a set, they check in. You can say what feels odd, and they deal with it. The message after you leave is normal here. That is why people who cannot risk a bad lash day on a shoot stay with one artist, and why I stopped shopping around.

## Who it suits

It suits you if you want one person who does lashes all day, and you are happy to book ahead. It also suits the north of the island, and anyone coming from Johor Bahru who would rather stop in Woodlands than go into town. It suits you if you are on camera often enough that "fine in person" is a low bar, and if you want a lift on quiet weeks and a named volume set when you need more.

Message ahead. The bio puts "Booked NOW" next to the phone number. I send the set I want, whether it is a first visit, and which day I can sit for the full appointment.

One keratin post is tagged **#doortodoorservice** as well as **#woodlands**. I go to the studio. If you need them to come to you, ask on that same number. The profile does not list the area or the fee, so I would confirm both before you plan around a home visit.

2. What the first-trial 20% covers, and what it leaves out.
3. How they want the set washed, and when to come back if something feels wrong.
4. Whether a door-to-door visit is available for your address, if the studio trip is the problem.
